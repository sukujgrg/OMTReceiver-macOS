Find. Choose. Play.

A clear view of
every source.

Keep the controls close and the picture where it belongs. OMTReceiver handles discovery, playback, and display selection in one focused Mac app.

  1. 01

    Browse what’s live.

    Compatible OMT sources appear automatically on your local network. Silent live thumbnails let you see what’s available before choosing your output.

  2. 02

    Choose your display.

    Use your Mac’s main screen or a connected display. Set your preferred destination in Settings; it’s remembered the next time you open the app.

  3. 03

    Click to play.

    Click a source to open full-quality video and audio on your chosen display. Click another to switch. Press Escape to stop output and return to your sources.

  1. Install. Download the notarized ZIP from the latest GitHub release, open it, and move OMTReceiver to Applications.
  2. Connect. Start a compatible OMT sender on your network, then open OMTReceiver.
  3. Play. Choose your fullscreen display in Settings and click a live source.

The built-in animated Test Pattern lets you try playback and display selection right away.

A little more to know.

Does it work with just one display?

Yes. Your Mac’s main screen is the default fullscreen destination, so an external display is optional. Use Show Sources to bring the browser back, or press Escape to stop playback.

Which OMT senders can I use?

Any compatible Open Media Transport sender. OMTReceiver is a generic receiver; eucaly, official OMT tools, and the OMT plugin for OBS are examples. You can also connect directly to a sender address or configure an OMT Discovery Server in Settings.

How do updates work?

OMTReceiver includes Sparkle for signed updates. Choose Check for Updates in the OMTReceiver menu, or manage automatic checks in Settings. Installation and restart stay under your control.

Where can I find help or build the app?

The project README covers setup, display behavior, format support, and building the complete Xcode project. If you run into a problem, open a GitHub issue with your macOS version and source format.
